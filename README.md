Vice Cover Studio turns your photos into GTA 6-style multi-panel cover art — crop and position photos across panels, add bold caption lettering, pick a sunset or neon colour grade, and export a share-ready PNG in 1080×1080, 1080×1350 or 1920×1080. Runs fully offline in the browser; your photos never leave your machine.

## Run it

```sh
python3 -m http.server 8080 --directory app
```

Then open http://localhost:8080 in a desktop browser, load a photo, pick a layout, write your caption, and export.
